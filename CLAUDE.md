# m-ko.de

Static trilingual site (DE, EN, PT) of Markus Kosmal.

## Wording rules

- **Never write "Ich bin Markus Kosmal"** (or "I'm / I am Markus Kosmal", "Sou o Markus Kosmal"). Markus is many things, but not his name. Use "Ich heiße Markus Kosmal", "My name is Markus Kosmal", "Chamo-me Markus Kosmal".
- German text uses neither "du" nor "Sie". Rephrase impersonally ("das Team", "die App", passive).
- No claims about certification, guarantees or legal compliance for reviews.
